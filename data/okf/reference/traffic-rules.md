# Traffic Rules and Penalties (Road Safety)

## Traffic signals and signs

Nepal traffic signals use standard international colors and shapes:

### Signals (traffic lights)

- **Red** — Stop. Do not proceed until the light turns green.
- **Yellow (Amber)** — Prepare to stop. If you are too close to stop safely, you may proceed with caution.
- **Green** — Proceed in the direction indicated. Check for pedestrians and cross traffic.

### Road signs

- **Regulatory signs** (circular, black on white/red): stop, give-way, noentry, no parking, speed limit.
- **Warning signs** (triangular, red border, black on yellow): bends, crossroads, pedestrian crossing, school zone.
- **Informational signs** (rectangular, blue/white): highway, hospital, parking, fuel.

## Speed limits (typical)

Speed limits vary by road type and location. Common reference:

- **Built-up urban areas:** 40–60 km/h (signs may indicate lower limits, e.g. 30 km/h near schools).
- **Rural highways:** 60–80 km/h (subject to signage and road conditions).
- **Highways (national roads):** up to 80 km/h (subject to signage).
- **School zones:** typically 30 km/h or as posted.

Always obey posted speed limits. Speed limits are legal maximums; reduce speed in poor weather, heavy traffic, or poor visibility.

## Right-of-way rules

- **Yield to pedestrians** at crossings (zebra crossings and signalized crossings).
- **Yield to traffic already in a roundabout** — enter only when there is a safe gap.
- **Yield to traffic coming from the right** at uncontrolled intersections (unless otherwise signaled).
- **Yield to emergency vehicles** (ambulance, fire, police) — pull over and let them pass.
- **Trams and public transport** — give way when required by signals and signs.

## Common traffic violations and penalties

Penalties are set by the Traffic Police and vary by violation. Common violations:

- **Speeding:** fines and possible license suspension (depends on excess speed).
- **Driving under the influence (DUI):** fines + license suspension/revocation.
- **Driving without a valid license:** fines + possible vehicle impoundment.
- **Driving without insurance:** fines + vehicle impoundment.
- **Trespassing on a restricted road:** fines.
- **Parking violations:** fines (e.g. parking in a "no parking" zone, blocking a drain).
- **Using a mobile phone while driving:** fines.
- **Not wearing a helmet (motorcycle):** fines.
- **Not wearing a seatbelt (car):** fines.

## Road conditions and safety

- **Narrow roads** — especially in rural areas; drive slowly and pass only when safe.
- **Poor road surfaces** — potholes, unmarked turns, and construction zones.
- **Cyclists and pedestrians** — share the road; exercise caution.
- **Wildlife crossings** — deer, cattle, and other animals may appear on roads, especially at dawn/dusk.
- **Inclement weather** — rain, fog, and snow (in Himalayan regions) require reduced speed and greater following distance.

## Red light / stop violations

- **Running a red light** — serious offense; carries heavy fines and possible jail time for repeat offenders.
- **Stopping at a yellow light** — not required if you cannot stop safely; always brake and check for cross traffic.
- **Failing to stop at a stop sign** — serious offense; may result in accidents and penalties.

## Emergency vehicles

- **Ambulance, fire engine, police** — yield the right of way at intersections and on roads.
- Pull over to the left side of the road and stop until the emergency vehicle passes.
- **Do not** follow or interfere with emergency vehicles.

## Reporting accidents / violations

- If you are involved in an accident:
  1. Stop immediately and check for injuries.
  2. Call the **Traffic Police** or **100** (emergency).
  3. Exchange details with other parties (names, contact, license/plate numbers).
  4. Do not move injured persons unless there is immediate danger.
  5. Report the incident to the Traffic Police station.
- To report a violation:
  1. Note the vehicle/plate number, location, and time.
  2. Contact the local Traffic Police office.
  3. Provide evidence (photos, video) where possible.

## Important notes for drivers

- **Always carry your driving license** while driving.
- **Wear a helmet** (motorcycles) and **seatbelt** (cars) — mandatory by law.
- **No mobile phone use** while driving.
- **No drinking and driving** — strictly enforced.
- **Speed limits are legal maximums**; reduce speed in poor conditions.
- **Keep a safe distance** from the vehicle ahead.
- **Use indicators** when turning or changing lanes.
- **Obey traffic signals and signs** at all times.

## Penalty enforcement

- **Traffic Police** (Traffic Police stations and check posts) enforce traffic rules.
- **Electronic tollgates and speed cameras** (where deployed) enforce speed limits.
- **Corruption / bribery** — do not pay bribes to traffic officers; report to the higher authorities.
- **Legal recourse** — a traffic ticket is an official notice; pay or contest through the legal process.
