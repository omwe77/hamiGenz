# Nepal Official Form Filling Guidelines

## General principles

1. **Read the entire form first** before filling anything. Understand what each field asks for.
2. **Use black or blue ink** for handwritten forms. Some offices accept only black.
3. **Write clearly** in Devanagari (Nepali) unless the form specifies English.
4. **Do not leave mandatory fields blank** — a form with missing mandatory fields may be rejected.
5. **Use the exact names** as they appear on your citizenship/passport — do not abbreviate or change spelling.
6. **Attach all required supporting documents** — a form without attachments is incomplete.
7. **Get it countersigned/stamped** where required — many forms need authority verification.

## Common fields on Nepal government forms

| Field label (Nepali) | Field label (English) | What to write |
|---|---|---|
| थर / नाम | Name / Surname | Your full name as on citizenship/passport |
| बाबुको नाम / पिताको नाम | Father's Name | Your father's full name |
| आमाको नाम | Mother's Name | Your mother's full name |
| नागरिकता नम्बर | Citizenship No. | Your citizenship certificate number |
| जन्म मिति | Date of Birth | DD/MM/YYYY format |
| लिंग | Gender | Male (पुलिंग) / Female (स्त्रीलिंग) |
| ठेगाना | Address | Your permanent address (house no, ward, VDC/Municipality, district) |
| सम्पर्क नम्बर | Contact No. | Your phone number |
| जारी मिति | Issue Date | Date of application or issue |
| हस्ताक्षर | Signature | Your signature |

## Form-filling mistakes to avoid

- **Misspelling names** — the name must match your identity document exactly.
- **Wrong date format** — Nepal uses DD/MM/YYYY. Do not write MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Illegible handwriting** — if a clerk cannot read it, it will be rejected.
- **Missing attachments** — each form specifies required attachments; do not skip any.
- **Blank mandatory fields** — if a field does not apply to you, write "उपलब्ध छैन" (not available) rather than leaving it blank.
- **Using the wrong form version** — always check you have the latest version of the form.
- **Submitting without stamp/signature** — unsigned forms are not accepted.

## Where to get forms

- District Administration Office (जिल्ला प्रशासन कार्यालय) — for citizenship, ID, passport-related forms.
- Ward office (वडा कार्यालय) — for local registration, demographic documents.
- Relevant ministry/office website — some forms are available for download.
- Embassy/consulate — for applicants outside Nepal.

## After filling

1. Review every field for accuracy.
2. Attach all documents listed in the form's checklist.
3. Submit to the designated office (check which office accepts the form).
4. Keep a copy of the filled form and all attachments.
5. Get a receipt/acknowledgment if provided.
6. Note the expected processing time and follow up if it exceeds the stated deadline.
